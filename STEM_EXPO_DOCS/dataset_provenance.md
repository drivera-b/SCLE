# SLCE Dataset Provenance

## 1. UCI Baseline Dataset
- Name: UCI Heart Disease (Cleveland subset)
- DOI: `10.24432/C52P4X`
- License: CC BY 4.0
- Local file in repo: `data/heart.csv`

SLCE uses this dataset for the Layer 1 logistic-regression classifier. The bundled cleaned file has 303 rows and the original clinical feature schema.

## 2. NHANES Lifestyle and Biomarker Extract
- Source: CDC/NCHS National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey
- Official overview: https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/continuousnhanes/overview.aspx?BeginYear=2017
- Cycle: 2017-2018
- Local processed file: `data/nhanes_lifestyle_biomarkers.csv`
- Rows after age/filter processing: 6,161
- Rebuild command: `python -m src.nhanes_dataset --build`

The reproducible pipeline downloads and merges official public XPT modules using the NHANES participant key (`SEQN`):

- Demographics and survey design weights
- Weekday/weekend sleep
- Adult and youth physical activity
- Blood pressure and pulse
- Body mass index
- Total and HDL cholesterol
- HbA1c
- Fasting glucose

The output retains survey weights, primary sampling units, and strata so future analyses can account for the NHANES complex survey design.

## How the datasets are used
- UCI remains the supervised baseline because it has a heart-disease label compatible with the existing classifier.
- NHANES supplies age/sex-relevant population percentiles and objective biomarker context.
- The datasets are not naively row-combined because they use different participants, schemas, sampling designs, and outcomes.
- Measured systolic blood pressure, total cholesterol, and fasting glucose can replace proxy/imputed UCI inputs in the app.
- HbA1c and BMI are displayed as reference context only; they are not assigned unsupported classifier weights.

## Synthetic data policy
Synthetic Monte Carlo paths are generated by the stochastic simulator and are always labeled as simulations. Synthetic records are not presented as observed medical data and are not used as ground-truth training labels.

## Reliability design
To keep demos stable on restricted networks:
- `data/heart.csv` is bundled in the repo.
- The processed NHANES research extract is bundled in the repo.
- `data/demo_sample.csv` is kept as fallback.
- If model artifacts are missing, SLCE can retrain from local data.
